    How it works

    Three sorting algorithms race on the same seeded array (`N=16`, `32`, or `64` as you level up). Each step is a compare or swap; the first lane to finish sorting wins.

    Odds come from each algorithm's real expected cost on this seed's inversion count and pivot balance. During the race, spend 40 CR on Boost (2× speed for 2s) or Jam (freeze the leading rival for 1.5s) once per round; each move trims your payout multiplier by 0.2×.

    Winning advances your array level (`16 → 32 → 64`) and builds your streak multiplier (`+0.25×` per win up to `2.0×`). Reaching 2,000 CR unlocks the 4th fighter, Heapforge (Heap Sort).
